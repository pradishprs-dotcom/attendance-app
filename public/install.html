Install the HRE app

Put Hospital Rental Equipment on your phone or computer like a normal app. No app store needed.

Open the app

Android phone (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app now above, or the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen) and confirm.
  4. The purple lotus icon appears on your home screen.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an up arrow).
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The icon appears on your home screen.

Windows / Mac computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open the site in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click Install app now above, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. It opens in its own window and can be pinned to the taskbar.
